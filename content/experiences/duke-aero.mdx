Duke AERO is a collegiate high-powered rocketry organization competing in the International Rocketry Engineering Competition (IREC). I own the full electrical stack for a custom Engine Control Unit powering a 2.5 kN liquid rocket engine — architecture, schematic capture, PCB layout, and power distribution — plus high-frequency sensor sampling and closed-loop valve/actuator control written in C++.

I also serve as lead electrical architect for Project Prometheus, an electric pump-fed bipropellant engine targeting a 2028 launch and the FAR-OUT challenge. I direct a subteam across PCB design, embedded firmware, and real-time control software covering pump control loops, active tank pressure regulation, and ground systems.
