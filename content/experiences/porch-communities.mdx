PORCH Communities is a nonprofit that mobilizes more than 1,500 volunteers nationwide. I scoped and delivered an eight-module volunteer onboarding curriculum, using Figma to design asynchronous, interactive training experiences.

Requirements came directly from active Neighborhood Coordinators, and I iterated the curriculum against their feedback — structuring content around measurable learning objectives and proposing a gamification system of badging, points, and leaderboards to improve retention.
