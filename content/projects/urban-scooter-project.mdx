## Main Frame Design

The deck is a three-part 1/8" 6061 aluminum plate. Carbon fiber tubing carries
the primary structural members — a high strength-to-weight ratio at low mass,
and the main reason the frame comes in as light as it does.

The braces joining plate to tube are the parts that had to be designed rather
than bought. Each was iterated in FEA to cut material anywhere the load case
didn't need it, ending at roughly 22 g per brace while still holding a safety
factor near 4 under a 1000 N static load.

## Collapsible Mechanism

Tubes of varying diameters slide on bushings, letting the frame shorten by more
than 30% for storage. The design work here is in the locking geometry: it has to
be light and it has to stay solid after repeated cycles, which are the two
requirements that pull hardest against each other.

## Full Mechanical Assembly

The full assembly brings together the deck, the telescoping front steering
column, and the wheels — a mix of off-the-shelf hardware and custom-fabricated
parts. The project is funded and currently in fabrication.
